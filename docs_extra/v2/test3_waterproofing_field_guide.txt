WATERPROOFING SYSTEMS — FIELD REFERENCE GUIDE
Flynn Group of Companies | Doc No: FG-WP-FIELD-2024 | Rev 2.0
=================================================================

PURPOSE
Quick reference for site supervisors and applicators on waterproofing
installation requirements for below-grade and plaza deck applications.

=================================================================
SECTION 1 — BELOW-GRADE WATERPROOFING
=================================================================

APPROVED MEMBRANE TYPES:
- Modified bitumen sheet membrane (SBS or APP)
  Standard: ASTM D6135 / CAN/CGSB 37.54
  Minimum thickness: 3.0 mm (self-adhered), 4.0 mm (torch-applied)

- Sheet-applied HDPE membrane
  Standard: ASTM D7408
  Minimum thickness: 1.0 mm

- Fluid-applied polyurethane membrane
  Standard: ASTM C836
  Minimum DFT (dry film thickness): 60 mils (1.5 mm)

SUBSTRATE REQUIREMENTS:
- Concrete must be cured minimum 28 days before membrane application
- Surface must be smooth, free of voids, fins, and sharp protrusions
- Moisture content: maximum 75% RH (test per ASTM F2170)
- Surface profile: CSP 3 minimum for fluid-applied systems
- All form tie holes and voids must be filled with non-shrink grout
  and allowed to cure before membrane application

APPLICATION CONDITIONS:
- Minimum ambient temperature: 10°C and rising
- Maximum ambient temperature: 35°C
- Do not apply in rain, snow, or when rain is forecast within 4 hours
- Protect freshly applied membrane from rain for minimum 24 hours

PROTECTION BOARD:
- Install protection board immediately after membrane inspection sign-off
- Minimum: 25 mm extruded polystyrene (XPS) or approved dimple mat
- Overlap joints minimum 100 mm, stagger vertical and horizontal joints
- Do not allow traffic on membrane without protection board in place

=================================================================
SECTION 2 — PLAZA DECK WATERPROOFING
=================================================================

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
All plaza deck waterproofing assemblies must achieve minimum 10-year
NDL warranty and comply with NRCA Plaza Deck Guidelines.

ASSEMBLY (bottom to top):
1. Structural slab — slope minimum 1.5% to drains
2. Primer coat — as required by membrane manufacturer
3. Waterproofing membrane — minimum 60 mil fluid-applied or
   approved sheet membrane
4. Protection course — 50 mm XPS insulation
5. Filter fabric — minimum 150 g/m2 non-woven geotextile
6. Drainage layer — minimum 25 mm dimple mat or gravel
7. Growing medium / pavers / ballast (as designed)

DRAIN INSTALLATION:
- Install clamping ring drains per manufacturer instructions
- Membrane must be fully bonded to clamping flange — no gaps
- Install secondary overflow drain at 50 mm above primary drain rim
- Test all drains with water before protection course is installed
- Document drain locations on as-built drawings

COMMON DEFICIENCIES TO AVOID:
1. Insufficient slope to drains — check with level before waterproofing
2. Membrane not carried up parapet walls minimum 200 mm above finish
3. Unclamped or improperly flashed drain penetrations
4. Protection board installed before membrane inspection
5. Traffic on unprotected membrane — causes punctures

SECTION 3 — INSPECTION & DOCUMENTATION
=================================================================

REQUIRED FORMS:
  FG-WP-PRE-001    Pre-application inspection checklist
  FG-WP-DFT-001    Dry film thickness log (fluid-applied only)
  FG-WP-TEST-001   Flood test / ELD test results
  FG-WP-POST-001   Post-installation sign-off

FLOOD TESTING:
- Flood test: pond minimum 50 mm of water for 48 hours
- Electronic Leak Detection (ELD): required for all systems
  exceeding 500 m2 and all rooftop planters
- Record all test results including weather conditions
